Port Sync

Pulls our GPA gate moves (AOLQ) with chassis, plate and driver from each gate ticket into the Yard App. It also sets GPA "container available" alerts for boxes on the Watch list.

Step 1: put the button on your bookmarks bar (one time)
⚓ Port Sync ← drag this button up onto your bookmarks bar

Can't see the bookmarks bar? Press ⌘ Shift B on a Mac (Ctrl Shift B on Windows). Don't click the button on this page. It only works inside GPA WebAccess.

Step 2: every time you want fresh port data
  1. Open GPA WebAccess and sign in yourself.
  2. Click ⚓ Port Sync on your bookmarks bar.
  3. The first time on a computer, type your personal Yard App code. It's saved on that computer only.
  4. Wait for Done (about a minute). If boxes are on the Watch list, tap Set GPA alerts to have GPA email us when they're available.

What it reads

Our truck transactions for today (AOLQ) and each gate ticket (EIR): chassis, plate, seal, weight, time. Moves on plates that aren't ours are skipped.

What it changes

Nothing at GPA except the Notify alerts you confirm. It never creates PINs. Everything it saves is logged with your name.

Troubleshooting
  • "Open GPA WebAccess, sign in…": you clicked it on another site. Go to WebAccess first.
  • "WebAccess signed out": WebAccess timed out. Sign in again and click Port Sync.
  • "Unknown code": your Yard App code was mistyped. Click Port Sync again and re-enter it.
  • Tags not on our list: a truck we don't have on file moved a box under AOLQ. Add it on the Port tab → Trucks.
