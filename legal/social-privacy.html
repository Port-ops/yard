2FF Yard Social – Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. Publisher: 2FF Logistics LLC, 116 Gulfstream Rd, Savannah, GA 31408 (an agency of America 1 Logistics), on behalf of 2FF Logistics LLC and 1 Stop Logistics LLC. Contact: admin@2f-logistics.com, (877) 940-2647.

"2FF Yard Social" is the internal app our companies use to publish posts to, and answer comments and messages on, our own Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts (2FF Logistics and 1 Stop Logistics). It is not used on anyone else's Page or account.

What we collect

When you comment on one of our posts or send a message to one of our Facebook Pages or Instagram accounts, Meta gives the app: your public name or username, the ID Meta assigns you for our Page or account, the text of your comment or message, the time it was sent, and the post it was on. We also keep the reply we sent. We do not receive your password, your friends list, or anything you did not send to us.

How we use it

Only to answer you: reply to your comment or message, send you the link you asked for (for example our job application or quote form), and pass driver or shipping inquiries to the right person on our team (recruiting, sales or the yard office). To draft replies quickly, the text of your comment or message is processed by an AI service (Anthropic) that does not use it to train its models. A person on our team can review or change any reply. We do not sell, rent or share this information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored and who can see it

In a private database hosted in the United States (Supabase on AWS), encrypted in transit and at rest. Only our staff who handle recruiting, sales and customer service can see it. Our Meta access key is kept in an encrypted server vault and never reaches a browser.

How long we keep it

Up to 24 months after your last message, then it is deleted. If you apply for a job or ask for a quote, that application or request is kept under our hiring or sales records.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can ask us to delete everything we hold about your comments and messages at any time. See how to delete your data. You can also delete your own comments on Facebook or Instagram yourself; we never hide, edit or delete anyone else's comments.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above.
