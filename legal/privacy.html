2FF Yard App – Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026. Publisher: 2FF Logistics LLC, 116 Gulfstream Rd, Savannah, GA 31408. Contact: rp@2f-logistics.com.

What we collect

When the company owner connects QuickBooks Online, the App receives OAuth tokens and reads the company's accounting data: invoices, payments, bills, bill payments, purchases, customers, vendors, chart of accounts and standard financial reports.

When the company owner connects a company bank account (1 Stop Logistics LLC or 2FF Logistics LLC) through Plaid, the App receives read-only access to that business account's name, last four digits, balances and transaction history. The App cannot move money. Plaid's handling of your bank login is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy; the owner gives consent in the Plaid window before any account is connected.

How we use it

Only to show the owners of 2FF Logistics LLC and 1 Stop Logistics LLC their own financial dashboard: revenue, profit, receivables, payables, cash and bank activity, and to check the books against the bank. We do not sell, rent or share this data, use it for advertising, or combine the two companies' books.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Data is stored in a private database hosted in the United States (Supabase, AWS us-east-1), encrypted in transit and at rest. QuickBooks and Plaid access tokens are kept in an encrypted secrets vault on the server and never reach a browser. Database rules allow only the company owner, signed in with multi-factor authentication, to read financial data. Every access and export is recorded in an audit log that cannot be edited.

Retention and deletion

Disconnecting QuickBooks or a bank account deletes its stored tokens immediately. Synced data is deleted on the owner's request to rp@2f-logistics.com.
