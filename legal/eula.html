The 2FF Yard App, including its QuickBooks Online connection ("the App"), is internal software. It is licensed only to 2FF Logistics LLC and 1 Stop Logistics LLC and their authorized personnel. It is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone else.

2. What the QuickBooks connection does

The App reads accounting data (invoices, payments, bills, expenses, customers, vendors, accounts and standard reports) from the QuickBooks Online companies of 2FF Logistics LLC and 1 Stop Logistics LLC, only after the company owner authorizes each company. It shows that data to the company owner. It does not create, change or delete accounting records and does not move money.

3. Access

Financial information is available only to the company owner, behind a separate sign-in with multi-factor authentication. The owner may disconnect QuickBooks at any time in the App (Owner > Integrations) or from QuickBooks Online.
