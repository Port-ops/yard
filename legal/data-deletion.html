How to delete your data

This applies to comments and messages you sent to the Facebook Pages or Instagram accounts of 2FF Logistics LLC or 1 Stop Logistics LLC, handled by our app "2FF Yard Social". See our privacy policy.

Ask us to delete it

  1. Email admin@2f-logistics.com with the subject "Delete my data", or send the words "delete my data" in a message to our Facebook Page or Instagram account.
  2. Tell us the name or username you used on Facebook or Instagram. Do not send passwords or ID numbers.
  3. We delete your comments, messages and our replies from our database within 30 days and confirm by email or message.

Do it yourself

You can delete your own comments on our posts, or remove our app's access in your Facebook settings under Settings & privacy > Settings > Apps and websites (only if you ever connected it, which customers normally do not).
